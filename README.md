Hospital Management Data Analysis
The data set contains information about patients,doctors and appointments in a hospital.it helps to understand patient demographics,doctor details,appointment trends,reason for visits and appointment status.The data is organized into three main sheets:Patients,Doctors and Appointments
  
Sheets used:
      1.Patients
      2.Doctors
      3.Appointments
 Business Questions:
      1.Which doctors have the highest number of appointments?
      2.What is the monthly trend of Appointments?
      3.What are the most common reasons For patient Visits?
      4.Which doctor Specializations receive the most Appointments?
      5.How are Appointments distributed by patient demographics?
      6.Which hospital branch has the highest no of appointments
